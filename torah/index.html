What this actually is

The text is the consonantal Torah of the Westminster Leningrad Codex — . Every letter you see is turned into a note by one of the mappings above; nothing is composed by hand.

Sefer Yetzirah follows the oldest Jewish text that divides the alphabet musically: three mothers (א מ ש) as bass pillars, seven doubles (ב ג ד כ פ ר ת) as the seven degrees of the scale, twelve simples as the twelve semitones.

Gematria reads each letter as a number: units low, tens in the middle, hundreds high. Alphabetical just walks Alef to Tav up the scale.

Cantillation is different in kind — it ignores the letters and chants the te‘amim, the accent marks that have carried the melody of the reading for a thousand years. The motifs here are a plain approximation of the Ashkenazi Torah trope, enough to hear the shape of a verse. A real reader does far more with them.

The modes are the Jewish prayer modes a reading actually lives in. Text from tanach.us.
