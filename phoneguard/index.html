PhoneGuard

Firewall and spyware scanner for Android. Block any app on Wi-Fi or mobile data (4G/5G) and find hidden apps someone may have put on your phone.

Download for Android Android 8.0 or newer · about 90 KB · no internet permission, so it can't send your data anywhere

Install

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open the file.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to Install unknown apps, then tap Install. If Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the app, choose Install anyway (it isn't from the Play Store).
  3. Open PhoneGuard, tap Turn on and accept the VPN request.
  4. Go to Scan and tap Scan my phone.
  5. Keep it on after restarts: Settings → Network → VPN → ⚙ next to PhoneGuard → Always-on VPN. Leave Block connections without VPN off.

What it does

  • Firewall: per-app switches for Wi-Fi and mobile data, plus a log of every connection a blocked app tried to make.
  • Spy app scan: checks against 600+ known stalkerware apps, finds apps without an icon, and apps that can read your screen, notifications or texts, or track your location.
  • Traffic snooping checks: extra security certificates, hidden proxies, unknown VPNs, USB or wireless debugging, missing screen lock, rooting, old updates.
  • Lock new apps: new apps get no internet until you allow them, with an alert showing what they can access.

Good to know

No app can catch every kind of spyware. Spyware sold to governments, or anything on a rooted phone, can hide from all apps. If you think someone close to you is spying on you and you feel unsafe, contact a local domestic-violence helpline before removing anything.
